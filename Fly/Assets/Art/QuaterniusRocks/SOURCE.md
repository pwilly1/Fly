# Quaternius rock study

These five rock FBX models and `Rocks.png` came from the user's downloaded Quaternius nature pack ZIP (`drive-download-20261110T232110Z-1-001.zip`). The creator lists the [Stylized Nature MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html) under CC0. Only the rock assets used in the Forest scene were imported.

The `Quaternius Cliff Study` group in `Forest.unity` is an editable test placement. The rock atlas material is matte and uses the pack's texture. The terrain textures in `CanyonLand` were generated separately for this game; `Rocks.png` is a model atlas, not a tileable terrain texture.
