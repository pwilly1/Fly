# Quaternius trees

Selected models and textures from the user's downloaded [Stylized Nature MegaKit](https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html), released under CC0 by Quaternius.

Drag the three assets in `ReadyPrefabs` into the Forest scene. They have textured bark, alpha-clipped two-sided leaves, and starting scales chosen for the canyon. The raw FBX files are kept alongside their source textures for later adjustments.
